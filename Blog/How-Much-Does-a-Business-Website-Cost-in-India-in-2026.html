How Much Does a Business Website Cost in India in 2026?

JK Click Craft• September 11, 2026• 8 min read
Approx. ₹8,000 to ₹5,00,000+ — what actually changes the price

If you're running a business in India and thinking about getting a website, one of the first questions that probably comes to mind is: "How much will a website actually cost?"

You may have seen website prices ranging from a few thousand rupees to several lakhs. That can make things confusing, especially if you're a small business owner or starting your first website.

The truth is, there isn't one fixed price for a business website. The cost depends on what you need, how complex the website is, the design, features, number of pages, and whether you need ongoing services such as SEO or maintenance. In this guide, let's break down the typical business website cost in India in 2026 in simple terms.

These are general ranges, not fixed prices. A website with 5 simple pages will naturally cost less than a custom platform with login systems, dashboards, payment integration, databases, and other advanced features.

1 Basic Business Website

A basic website is usually suitable for small businesses, freelancers, local service providers, professionals, and new startups. It may include pages such as:

  • Home
  • About Us
  • Services
  • Gallery or Portfolio
  • Contact Us
A basic business website in India may typically cost around
₹8,000 – ₹20,000 (approx.)
Example: A local catering business may simply need a professional website that displays its menu, services, photos, contact details, and enquiry options. You don't necessarily need a complicated system when your main goal is simply to build trust and make it easy for customers to contact you.

2 Professional Business Website

If you're looking for something more polished and customized, a professional business website may be a better choice. These websites generally include:

  • Custom UI/UX design
  • Responsive mobile design
  • Multiple business pages
  • Contact and enquiry forms
  • WhatsApp integration
  • Google Maps
  • Social media integration
  • Basic SEO setup
  • Optimized images and content
  • Professional animations or interactions
A professional website may cost approximately
₹20,000 – ₹50,000 (approx.)

For many established small and medium-sized businesses, this is often the sweet spot. You're not just paying for a few web pages — you're investing in how your business is presented online.

3 E-commerce Website

Planning to sell products online? Then you'll need more than a normal business website. An e-commerce website may require:

  • Product management
  • Categories
  • Shopping cart
  • Customer accounts
  • Online payments
  • Order management
  • Inventory management
  • Shipping options
  • Discount/coupon systems
  • Email or WhatsApp notifications
Depending on the number of products and required features, an e-commerce website can range from around
₹40,000 – ₹2,00,000+ (approx.)

4 Custom Web Applications

This is where website development can become significantly more advanced.

Example: Imagine a business that needs employee management + customer management + reports + login system + admin dashboard + database + file management. That's no longer just a normal website — it's a custom web application.
Depending on the features and complexity, custom web applications can start around
₹75,000 – ₹5,00,000+ (approx.)

The final cost depends heavily on users, modules, security requirements, integrations, database structure, dashboards, and business workflows.

Why Does Website Price Vary So Much?

You might wonder why one developer quotes ₹10,000 (approx.) while another quotes ₹50,000 (approx.) for what appears to be the same website.

  1. Design: A ready-made template and a completely custom-designed website are not the same.
  2. Number of Pages: A 5-page website requires much less work than a 20-page website with unique layouts and content.
  3. Features: Booking systems, payment gateways, login/register, admin dashboards, product management, APIs, chatbots, and databases can significantly increase development time and cost.
  4. Content: Some businesses already have professional images, videos, text, menus, and branding. Others need help preparing everything.
  5. SEO: A website being online doesn't automatically mean people will find it on Google. SEO involves keyword research, technical optimization, page structure, metadata, content, local SEO, and ongoing improvements. Don't Choose a Website Based Only on Price

This is probably the most important thing to remember.

A ₹10,000 (approx.) website isn't necessarily bad. And a ₹1,00,000 (approx.) website isn't automatically good. The important question is: Does the website solve your business problem?

For one business, a simple ₹15,000 (approx.) website may be completely sufficient. For another business, spending ₹75,000 (approx.) on a custom website could make much more sense.

Think about what you want your website to achieve: more enquiries, Google visibility, online sales, bookings, or a business management system. Your requirements should determine your budget — not the other way around.

What About Domain and Hosting?

Website development isn't always the only expense.

  • Domain: Your website address, such as yourbusiness.com.
  • Hosting: The server where your website files are stored.
  • SSL: Provides HTTPS security for your website.
  • Maintenance: Updates, backups, security monitoring, changes, and technical support.

Some developers include these in their packages, while others charge separately. Before starting a project, always ask: "What exactly is included in the quoted price?"

Is a Website Worth It for a Small Business?

In most cases, yes — if it's built with a clear purpose.

When customers discover a new business, they may search for it on Google, check its website, look at its services, see photos, read information, and then decide whether to contact them.

A professional website gives your business a place that you control and can help establish credibility, visibility, and trust online. It can also work 24/7, giving potential customers information even when you're not available to answer their questions.

Final Thoughts

So, how much does a business website cost in India in 2026?

A basic website may start around ₹8,000–₹20,000 (approx.), while a professional website may fall around ₹20,000–₹50,000 (approx.). E-commerce websites and custom web applications can cost considerably more depending on their requirements.

But don't focus only on the number. Focus on what you're getting for that investment. A good website should not simply look attractive — it should represent your business professionally, work well on mobile devices, make it easy for customers to contact you, and support your business goals.
